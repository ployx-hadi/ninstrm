Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the Ninja Stream app for Android phones, tablets and Android TV (package name com.therazgames.ninjastream), who receives it, and what choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer of Ninja Stream.

In short

  • Ninja Stream has no accounts. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or password.
  • The app does not ask for access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • The app shows ads and uses analytics. The advertising and analytics services built into the app collect device identifiers and usage information, as described below.
  • You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings and switch notifications off at any time.

1. Information that is collected

Information you give us

None inside the app. If you write to us by e-mail, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you put in your message, and we use them only to answer you.

Information collected by services built into the app

Ninja Stream includes software from other companies for advertising, analytics and notifications. These services collect information automatically while the app is used:

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Advertising: CAS.AI ad mediation and the ad networks it works with, including Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address (from which a general location such as country or city can be worked out), device details (model, operating system, language, screen size), app version, which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic data To show ads, limit how often the same ad appears, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Where the law allows it or you have agreed, ads may be personalised.
Google Analytics for Firebase An app instance identifier, the advertising ID where available, device and app details, general location (country or region) and usage events such as app opens, sessions and screens viewed To understand how the app is used and to improve it
Firebase Cloud Messaging A notification token that identifies this installation of the app To deliver notifications about games and app news
Firebase Remote Config An installation identifier and basic app details To load the app's settings

Technical data when the app connects to servers

  • Our server. The app downloads its schedule of games, its settings and the list of streams from our server. Like any website, the server receives your IP address, the time of the request and basic technical details of the connection.
  • Other servers. Video streams, team logos and other images, and live scores are loaded directly from servers run by other parties. Those servers receive your IP address and basic technical details of your device and connection, in the same way as when you open a web page. We do not control them, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • Name lookup. If your network's own name lookup (DNS) fails, the app can ask the public DNS services of Google and Cloudflare for the address of a server. These services then see which server name was looked up.

Information kept only on your device

The app stores its settings and some downloaded data (for example the list of games and team colours) on your device so that it works faster. The app also checks on the device whether the device is rooted and whether ads are being blocked. The results of these checks are used inside the app and are not sent to us.

2. Permissions

  • Internet, network state and Wi-Fi state: to load games, streams and ads, and to know whether the device is online.
  • Notifications: to show notifications. On Android 13 and later the app asks you first, and you can refuse.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising and analytics services listed above.

The app and the services built into it also use a few standard technical permissions, for example to receive notifications and to finish tasks in the background. None of them gives access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

3. How the information is used

  • To run the app: load the schedule, scores and streams, and play video.
  • To show and measure advertising, which pays for the app.
  • To send notifications, if you allow them.
  • To understand in general how the app is used, find and fix problems, and improve the app.
  • To protect the app and its services against misuse and fraud.

We do not use this information to work out who you are, and we do not build a personal profile of you.

4. Who receives information

We do not sell your information for money. Information is received by:

  • The services listed in section 1, which collect it directly through the app: CAS.AI and its advertising partners, and Google (AdMob and Firebase). They process it under their own privacy policies:
    • CAS.AI Privacy Policy
    • Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
    • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Operators of the other servers the app connects to (streams, images, live scores, name lookup), as described in section 1.
  • Authorities, where a law or a valid legal request requires it.

Advertising partners may use identifiers such as the advertising ID to show ads based on your interests. Some laws, including those of some US states, call this "sharing" or "selling" personal information. Section 5 explains how to opt out.

5. Your choices

  • Consent to personalised ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your choice when it starts, before personalised ads are shown.
  • Advertising ID. In your device settings (usually Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation. Ads are then still shown, but they are not based on your interests.
  • Notifications. You can switch them off in the device settings for the app.
  • Stop all collection. Uninstalling the app stops all collection through the app and removes the data it stored on your device.

6. Keeping and deleting data

  • We do not run user accounts and we do not keep a database of the app's users.
  • Our server's connection logs are kept only for as long as they are needed for security and for finding faults, and are then deleted.
  • E-mails you send us are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request.
  • Data collected by the advertising, analytics and notification services is kept by those companies for the periods set out in their own policies (see section 4).
  • Data stored on your device is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

To ask about your data or to request deletion, write to us at the address in section 12. Because the app has no accounts, we may need the advertising ID of your device to pass a request on to the services that hold data linked to it.

7. Security

The app requests its own data from our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No way of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Children

Ninja Stream is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

9. Your rights in some regions

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. You have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Personalised advertising is based on your consent where the law requires consent. The other uses described in this policy are based on our legitimate interest in running, protecting and improving the app. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

United States. Depending on the state you live in, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to have it deleted, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. You can opt out with the choices in section 5. We do not treat you differently for using these rights.

10. International processing

The companies named in this policy operate in many countries. Your information may be processed outside the country you live in, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in those companies' policies.
